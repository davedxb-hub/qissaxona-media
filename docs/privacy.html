Privacy Policy — Qissaxona Publisher

Last updated: 17 November 2026.

Qissaxona Publisher is used only by the Qissaxona team to publish videos we created to accounts we own (Instagram @qissaxona, our YouTube channel and our TikTok account).

  • Data we access: the OAuth tokens our own accounts grant, basic account info (to confirm the right account) and the status and IDs of the videos we upload.
  • Storage: tokens are stored encrypted as GitHub Actions secrets. Video IDs, links and upload times are kept in a private publishing log.
  • Sharing: we do not sell or share any data. We do not collect data about viewers or other users.
  • Deletion: revoking the app's access in the platform's settings stops all access; we then delete the stored tokens.

Contact: via Instagram direct message to @qissaxona.
